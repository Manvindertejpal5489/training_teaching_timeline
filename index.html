Notes

  1. Each intern's 12-week posting with the department is charted: three consecutive 4-week rotation blocks — CUHA, CRHA and IPC in one of three orders — per the signed Intern 2021 Batch roster (July 2026 – June 2027). The rest of the internship year is with other departments and is left blank. Lanes are grouped by the six staggered rotation groups; every group's three blocks overlap the next group's first block.
  2. The CUHA block is split into two fortnights per the monthly CUHA intern roster: one at UPHC Sai Baba Nagar and one at the Sanjeevani Clinic, Berkheda Pathani ("Barkhera" on the roster) — the alphabetical first half of each block's CUHA cohort starts at Sai Baba, the second half at Barkhera, and both centres stay staffed throughout. The swap falls on day 15 of a block (19 May for Block 12; 13 Jun for the three-week Block 13).
  3. From 14 September 2026 the CRHA block is posted site-wise, because the two rural centres are reached by two different vehicles. Each block is cut into three periods and the block's CRHA cohort into three alphabetical sub-groups; each sub-group takes one period at CHC Goharganj and two at RHC Chiklod, so every intern sees both and about a third of the cohort is at Goharganj on any day. Periods and sub-groups both carry the remainder at the front — a 28-day block runs 10/9/9 days, a 15-intern cohort splits 5/5/5. Peak daily strength is 10 at Chiklod and 5 at Goharganj. Block 3 (26 Aug – 22 Sep 2026) is split only for its third period, 14–22 Sep, since 26 Aug – 13 Sep was issued as undifferentiated CRHA; Blocks 1 and 2 are unchanged.
  4. The Ayush week ("IPC/Ayush for 3rd week", "CUHA/Ayush for first week" …) is drawn as a striped bar carved out of the block named on the roster; the tooltip and the text table give the week number and the block. Groups 1–5 take it in their third block, Group 6 in its first.
  5. Nursing students at the IPC. The Immunization station of the B.Sc (Hons) Nursing community-posting micro plans is held at the IPC, so on those days the Current box lists the day's nursing students in the IPC row after the interns ("IPC · 12 interns + 2 nursing students"), by name and group; hover or focus a name for the student's serial number in the group. One or two a day: CHN-II Groups 1, II and III in their Barkhera and Sai Baba blocks (31 Aug – 14 Sep, 5–17 Oct and 9–21 Nov 2026) and CHN-I Groups I–III in their urban and rural postings (16 Dec 2026 – 8 Jan, 13 Jan – 6 Feb and 10 Feb – 5 Mar 2027). A student the micro plan prints at two stations on one day is counted at the first. Nursing students are not drawn on this chart; their postings are on the Nursing view.
  6. Block dates follow the signed roster: 1–28 Jul, 29 Jul–25 Aug, 26 Aug–22 Sep, 23 Sep–20 Oct, 21 Oct–17 Nov, 18 Nov–15 Dec, 16 Dec–12 Jan, 13 Jan–9 Feb, 10 Feb–9 Mar, 10 Mar–6 Apr, 7 Apr–4 May, 6 May–1 Jun and 2–23 Jun. The monthly CUHA rosters print Block 12 from 5 May; the signed roster's 6 May is used here and 5 May is unassigned. The October 2026 monthly roster leaves the Barkhera column blank for 21–31 Oct; the fortnight rule above (which every other month follows) is applied for those days.
  7. Interns are listed by name as on the roster; hover or focus a name for the roll number. Six roll numbers carry the prefix 2100995 exactly as printed on the signed roster. Serial numbers 020, 030 and 095 of the batch do not appear on the roster.

Notes

  1. Outreach camp posting (31 Aug – 11 Dec 2026): one nursing student joins the outreach mobile van each listed day, reporting to the CFM resident on outreach at 9.15 am at the van's location. The calendar names the student per date; a coloured dot marks the batch. "No student posted" reproduces the dashes in the roster; blank dates (Sundays, holidays) are not on the roster. 7 Nov is held for any M.Sc student joining through open counselling.
  2. CHN-I community posting (B.Sc (Hons) Nursing III Year, Batch 2024): three groups in turn, each starting with two field-visit days (Sanchi dairy plant & water purification plant; India waste management), then the urban field posting at Berkheda Patani, Saibaba Nagar and the 1100 quarters, then the rural posting at Chiklod. Bars show the posting days exactly as listed in the circular (gaps are Sundays and holidays); 9 am – 5 pm, field posting 9.30 am – 1 pm, afternoon in the lab.
  3. CHN-II community posting (4th Year B.Sc (Hons) Nursing, Batch 2023; 31 Aug – 10 Dec 2026): the same three groups as the camp roster, each for 27 posting days in five blocks — Berkheda Patani (Days 1–6), Saibabanagar / 1100 quarters / Shahpura (Days 7–12), Chiklod (Days 13–18), Goharganj (Days 19–24) and Umraoganj (Days 25–27). Students report to the CHN lab at 9 am for the day's briefing; no vehicle is provided for the peripheral postings; for the hospital units they report to the nursing officer in charge. "Misrod ATF" is to be read as the psychiatry OPD ATF; at Umraoganj the four hospital units take the PHC group if the PHC is not open yet. Group 1's Days 25–26 (29–30 Sep) are marked * on the circular: hospital units posting, as the 2022 batch has its final examinations. Each day's outreach camp student (calendar above) is drawn from the group on posting.
  4. Department focus. Both micro plans are reproduced in full, but only part of each is catered for by the Department of Community and Family Medicine: Sanjeevani Clinic, Barkhera Patani (urban field practice area (home visits, AWW)); UPHC Sai Baba Nagar and the 1100 quarters (urban field practice area (home visits, AWW)); PHC and RHC Chiklod (rural field practice area (HWC / AWW Tilandi, home visits)); CHC Goharganj (rural field practice area (HWC, home visits)); Immunisation & Prophylaxis Clinic (IPC) (the Immunization station of both micro plans, at the same clinic as the interns on their IPC block). Those stations are shown in full with the place named under the printed heading and listed first in the Current box; the stations outside the department's postings — DOTS, ICTC, ART, ATF / Misrod ATF, Yoga, Diet OPD, Dental OPD, Integrated clinic, Oncology day care, Eye bank, Milk bank, Skin bank, and the whole Umraoganj block — are muted in the tables, folded into one "Other units" line in the Current box, and drawn grey on the CHN-II chart.
